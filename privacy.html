Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FW Ecom Global LLC ("FW Ecom", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information on this website (fwecom.com) and in FW Ecom, our internal operations application available at app.fwecom.com.

1. Who we are

FW Ecom Global LLC is a Wyoming limited liability company with its principal office at 8200 NW 41st Street, 2nd Floor, Doral, FL 33166, United States. For any privacy question, contact us at admin@fwecom.com.

2. This website

fwecom.com is an informational website. It has no user accounts, contact forms or advertising trackers. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you.

3. The FW Ecom application

app.fwecom.com is a private tool used only by FW Ecom and its authorized team members to run our e-commerce operations. It is not offered to the public. To use it, a team member signs in with an account created by an administrator, protected by a password and two-factor authentication.

We store the information needed to run our operations, such as team member names, email addresses, activity logs, store and order data, and the credentials of the service providers we connect to. Credentials and other sensitive fields are encrypted at rest.

4. Google user data

Team members may connect a Google account that manages our own Google Ads accounts. When they do, the application requests:

  • Google Ads (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords): to read reporting data from the Google Ads accounts we manage, namely campaign spend and performance, account status and policy or suspension notices.
  • Basic profile (openid, email): to show which Google account is connected.

We use this data only to display advertising spend, profitability and account health inside FW Ecom for our own stores. The application does not create, modify or delete anything in Google Ads.

Storage and protection. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and are accessible only to our servers. Reporting data is stored in our database, hosted in the United States, with restricted access.

Sharing. We do not sell Google user data. We do not share, transfer or disclose it to third parties, except as needed to provide the features described above, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to affected users.

No advertising or AI training. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not used to build or improve general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine learning models, and is not read by humans except with the user's consent, for security purposes, or to comply with the law.

Limited Use. FW Ecom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep information only while it is needed for our operations or to meet legal obligations. A connected Google account can be disconnected at any time in the application, which deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any data related to you, email admin@fwecom.com.

6. Security

We use HTTPS for all traffic, encryption at rest for credentials and tokens, two-factor authentication for team members, role-based permissions and audit logs of sensitive actions.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
